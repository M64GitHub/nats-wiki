# CNCF — NATS project page

The foundation's own record of NATS. One sentence on it is load-bearing and the rest is a live
metrics dashboard, so this summary keeps the durable fact and dates the volatile ones.

## Key claims

- **"NATS was accepted to CNCF on March 15, 2018 at the Incubating maturity level."** That is the
  governance fact: NATS is a CNCF project, **Incubating**, since 2018-03-15. The CNCF's own
  definitions on the same page: *Graduated* — "stable, widely adopted, and production ready";
  *Incubating* — "used successfully in production by a small number users with a healthy pool of
  contributors"; *Sandbox* — "Experimental projects not yet widely tested in production".
- CNCF's one-line description: "NATS.io is a connective technology for distributed systems … True
  multi-tenancy makes NATS ideal for SaaS and self-healing and scaling technology allows for topology
  changes anytime with zero downtime."
- **Project Insights, as of the 2026-08-31 fetch** (LFX Insights; these move continuously and should
  be re-read rather than quoted later): Health Score **Fair (67)**, measuring "contributors,
  development, popularity, and security"; **6,482 total contributors**, +9% year on year.

## Practical takeaways

- **Incubating, not Graduated.** For an architect writing a technology-selection document, this is
  the row that gets asked about. It says the project is governed by a neutral foundation and has
  passed CNCF's incubation bar — not that it has cleared graduation. It is a governance status, not a
  statement about whether NATS is production-ready, which the release history and the
  [[nats-server]] security audit speak to instead.
- **The metrics are a dashboard, not a fact.** Health score and contributor counts change weekly.
  Anything citing them needs the date attached, which is why they are dated here.
