## Notes on provenance

**Closed with zero comments and no answer chosen.** The question is precise, operationally
consequential — it decides whether a `nats server check server` CPU threshold is right or off by the
ratio between host cores and container allocation — and nobody replied.

The reporter's own observation that `"cores"` reports the **host's** logical CPUs rather than the
container's vCPU allocation is consistent with the source: `Varz.Cores` is `runtime.NumCPU()` and
nothing in `monitor.go` or `server/pse/` consults a cgroup CPU quota.

The question itself is answerable from the source, and the answer is "neither": `cpu` is a percentage
of **one core**, so `100.0` is one core fully consumed. See
`raw/nats-server-src/monitoring-observed-v2.14.6.md`.
