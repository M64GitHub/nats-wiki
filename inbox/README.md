# inbox/

Working files that are **not** wiki pages: triage tables of contents (`<collection>-toc.md`),
scout results (`scout-<topic>-<date>.md`), ingest plans (`plan-<what>-<date>.md`), the kickoff
record, and `extracts/` (git-ignored) for article text pulled out of big raw files while reading.
Delete or mark plans as done when they are finished; keep scout files, they record what was
considered and why.
